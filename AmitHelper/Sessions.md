**2026-11-06 (night) - FOLLOW-UP TEST, both mice clicking at the same time (strict first-with-first pairing per button; script in the session scratchpad, `mouse_match_test.ps1`).** 107 clicks in total: **107 of 107 matched to a mouse in order, 0 mouse reports left over.** Phase 1 (both left buttons together): 17 from the wireless-receiver mouse and 16 from the gaming mouse. Phase 3 (gaming mouse's left button HELD, wireless mouse clicked about 8+ times): 1 press from the gaming mouse and 12 from the wireless mouse, which is exactly what was done, so attribution stayed correct while two buttons overlapped. **5 pairs of clicks from different mice landed within 0.3 to 0.63 ms of each other.** The mouse report arrived after the click in 101 of 107 cases (typical 0.3 ms) and before it in 6 (as much as 16 ms before: the hook is occasionally late); 4 pairs were more than 5 ms apart for that reason. One more software-sent (nameless) click appeared, most likely Amit Live's right-button replay. **What the test cannot prove:** which of two clicks only about half a millisecond apart belongs to which mouse (there is no ground truth for that moment). **Rules for the real program:** (1) keep a FIRST-IN-FIRST-OUT queue of unused mouse reports PER BUTTON; a click takes the oldest one (wait up to about 3 ms if none yet; reports can also come up to about 20 ms early); (2) **if two clicks of the same button from different mice come within about 5 ms of each other, treat BOTH as unsure and let both through untouched** (the primary mouse can never be hurt by a mix-up); (3) never block or remap a click that cannot be matched, nor one flagged as sent by software.
