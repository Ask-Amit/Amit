Are You Saved? — A Scriptural Self-Examination

How this works: This examination draws from a bank of salvation-focused questions — each one directly tests your relationship with Yeshua, your understanding of sin, your walk of obedience, and your readiness to endure. Knowledge-only questions have been removed; every question here is about whether your life aligns with what scripture says about being saved. Each time you take it, 20 questions are randomly selected, so the test is different every session. Each question has 4 options — only one aligns with the full scriptural witness. Scripture references include clickable links to both a modern translation (ESV) and a literal translation with Strong's numbers (Blue Letter Bible). Answer honestly. After the 20 questions, answer one final question about Yeshua.

Scoring: Each correct answer = 1 point (max 20). Your percentage reflects alignment with the scriptural pattern. The final Yeshua question then adjusts the result — because who He is determines everything.

Important Disclaimer

No quiz can determine your eternal destiny — only God knows the heart (1 Sam 16:7, Jer 17:10). This tool is for self-examination as Paul commanded in 2 Corinthians 13:5. A low score is not condemnation — it is an invitation to study. A high score is not a guarantee — it is encouragement to continue. This is a mirror, not a verdict.
