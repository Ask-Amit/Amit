The 10 Independent Evidence Categories

These categories were selected because they are testable by any honest investigator regardless of faith. They do not presuppose any religious framework. They are the same categories a historian, archaeologist, philosopher, or scientist would use to evaluate any truth claim.

1. Fulfilled Predictive Prophecy

Specific predictions written before the event, verifiable by external dating, confirmed by archaeology and history. The gold standard — no human fabrication can reliably predict the future at scale.

2. Historical Verifiability

Are central claims confirmable by hostile, neutral, or independent sources? External corroboration — not just internal testimony — is the historian's standard.

3. Manuscript Reliability

Quantity of manuscripts, proximity to originals, and independent textual streams determine how confidently we know what the original texts said.

4. Internal Consistency

Does the religion contradict itself across its own texts, written by different authors over centuries? Divine authorship implies coherence.

5. Cosmological Explanatory Power

Does the religion explain the origin of the universe, fine-tuning of physical constants, consciousness, and the existence of objective morality — without self-contradiction?

6. Central Resurrection Claim

Does the religion make a falsifiable historical claim about death being conquered? Is that claim the best explanation of all known historical facts?

7. Coherent Account of Evil

Does the religion explain why evil and suffering exist without making God their author or rendering suffering meaningless? Is the explanation consistent with observable reality?

8. Moral Framework Coherence

Are moral commands derivable from the stated nature of the religion's ultimate reality? Does the system produce human flourishing when genuinely applied?

9. Founder Credibility

What do external sources tell us about the founder? Did the founder's life match the teaching? Were the founder's specific predictions accurate? Did the founder die for their own claim?

10. Transformed Lives Under Pressure

Does genuine practice produce documented transformation — especially in extremity: terminal illness, addiction, persecution, martyrdom? This measures sincerity and depth of conviction, not truth by itself — a person can be sincerely mistaken. What it rules out is cynicism: cheap, self-serving belief does not typically survive contact with real suffering.

Narrative Analysis — What the Evidence Actually Shows

Biblical Faith — Why It Leads on Every Testable Category

Prophecy: No other religious tradition has anything comparable to the Hebrew prophetic corpus. Over 300 specific Messianic predictions — naming Bethlehem (Micah 5:2), describing betrayal for 30 pieces of silver (Zechariah 11:12-13), detailing crucifixion symptoms (Psalm 22, written 1,000 years before Rome invented crucifixion), giving a mathematically precise timeline to the cutting off of the Anointed One (Daniel 9:24-26). These texts are independently dated by the Dead Sea Scrolls to before the events they describe. No other world religion has pre-written, externally verified, chronologically datable fulfilled prophecy at this density and specificity.

Resurrection: The resurrection of Yeshua is the most investigated event claim in ancient history. The minimal facts accepted by the majority of critical scholars regardless of faith: Yeshua died by crucifixion (confirmed independently by Tacitus, Josephus, Pliny the Younger, the Babylonian Talmud); the tomb was empty (opponents acknowledged this — they claimed the body was stolen, not that He remained buried); the disciples genuinely believed they encountered the risen Yeshua; Paul and James — both hostile or indifferent before — became believers on the basis of claimed appearances; the disciples died for this specific claim. The alternatives (swoon, hallucination, legend, stolen body) each fail under examination. The resurrection is the best historical explanation of all known data — not by faith assumption, but by the standard historians use for any ancient event.

Manuscript: The New Testament has over 5,800 Greek manuscripts and 24,000+ total copies. The earliest papyri date within 25-50 years of the originals. No other ancient document approaches this. Caesar's Gallic Wars: 10 manuscripts, 1,000-year gap. Homer's Iliad: 643 manuscripts, 400-year gap. No scholar doubts we have Caesar's words. The NT text is established with greater certainty than any other ancient document. The Old Testament is confirmed by the Dead Sea Scrolls showing 99%+ accuracy over 1,000 years of transmission.

Cosmology: Biblical faith provides coherent answers to questions every worldview must face: the universe had a beginning (Genesis 1:1, confirmed by Big Bang cosmology and the Borde-Guth-Vilenkin theorem) — whatever begins has a cause; fine-tuning of physical constants (purposeful design by a designer); consciousness (humans bear the image of a conscious, personal God — imago Dei explains why consciousness exists in a material universe); objective morality (rooted in God's unchanging nature, not human preference — explains why "the Holocaust was wrong" is a fact, not an opinion).
