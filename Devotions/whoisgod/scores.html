Denomination Scorecard — Covenant Alignment from His Word

Amit independently identified 17 scoring categories by scanning every major scriptural witness for repeated markers of covenant faithfulness. A category required confirmation from multiple witnesses (Torah, Prophets, Yeshua, Apostles, Revelation, Millennial prophecy, additional texts) to qualify. The human requester did not supply these categories. Click a denomination name to learn what that church is and where it came from. Click any score cell for specific reasoning. Click any column header to see why that category was included and the scriptures that mandate it.

Scores reflect movement-level doctrine. Individual congregations and believers may differ. Total possible: 170 (17 categories × 10 pts each).

How a score is built. Every cell is scored 1 to 10 against that category's stated scripture. Anchors: 10 = the movement's own published doctrine and practice match the text with no meaningful gap; 8-9 = strong, with a named gap; 5-7 = partial or divided; 2-4 = most of the movement departs, with some credit; 1 = the movement rejects, or has no concept of, what the text requires. Scores reflect published movement-level doctrine and practice, not individual members. Where a movement publishes no position I score the evangelical default and say so. Totals are the sum of 17 cells out of 170; each church's percentage is its total over 170, shown as a percent match to Scripture and colored green at 80% and above, yellow from 60% to 79%, and red below 60%. Every cell carries its reasoning (click it), every change is dated, and for the four most contested categories (Sabbath, Feasts, Torah, Diet) the texts set against the position are answered in that column's header popup. If you can show from scripture and the record that a score is wrong, bring it. Groups were added by size and public profile; 26 were added on 2026-10-04.

Reading this scorecard: Yeshua established a hierarchy — love God with all your heart, soul, and mind; love your neighbor as yourself. Everything else hangs on these two (Matt 22:37-40). The Love & Foundation column (headed "Love") measures whether a tradition's entire practice flows from that root. Use it as the lens through which all other scores are read.
