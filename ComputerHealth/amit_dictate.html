How Dictate works

1. StartChoose Dictate on the Amit ring. If you are in a text box, Amit types your words straight into it, and one right-click stops it. If you are not in a text box, this window opens in the top right of your screen. While this window is open, click into a text box and wait a second: everything here is typed there, this window closes, and your dictation carries on in that box. Place does the same when you want to choose the exact spot yourself. The microphone blinks green while Amit is listening. Click the microphone to pause, and again to listen.
2. TalkYour words appear in the Message. Everything is kept on this computer as you go, so nothing is lost.
3. Split into partsPress + to start a numbered section (#1, then #2, and so on). Your next words go in that section. Press − to remove only the section you are in. You can bring it back with Undo. You can also say “next item”.
4. PlacePress Place, then left-click where the message should go: a program, a website, Ask Amit, anywhere with a text box. Only the Message is sent, not the Subject. Press Esc to cancel. If you click something that is not a text box, Amit tells you and waits for another click.
5. Save to JournalSends the whole dictation (subject, message and sections) to your Amit Journal. If you change it later, it is marked “changed since” and you can save again.
6. Go backRecent lists earlier dictations. Open and change brings one back. + New starts a fresh one.
7. Throw awayDiscard removes the dictation you are in and keeps nothing.
8. SettingsChoose how long and how many dictations are remembered, and how the microphone shows it is listening.
