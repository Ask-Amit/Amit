Amit Phone

Your phones and iPads become part of your computer: a touchpad, your own buttons, a camera and a way to ask Amit. They work on your home Wi-Fi and belong to you: each one is linked to the Amit account you are signed in with on this computer, so nobody else's phone shows up here and yours never shows up for them.

Nobody is signed in to Amit on this computer. Sign in to the Hub here first: a phone connects only to the account that is signed in.

What your phone gets

MouseMove the pointer, click, scroll and zoom with your finger. Touchpad or Direct.
NavigationYour own Helper buttons, the Helper bar, the Amit menu and the Aurora, one tap away.
PhotoTake a picture; it opens on the computer in Show Amit.
Ask AmitType or speak a question; the answer shows on the phone and can be spoken.
More will be added here as they are built.
