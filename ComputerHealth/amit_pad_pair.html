Amit Phone

Your phone, or an iPad, becomes part of your computer: a touchpad, your own buttons, a camera and a way to ask Amit. It works on your home Wi-Fi and belongs to you: it is linked to the Amit account you are signed in with on this computer, so nobody else's phone shows up here and yours never shows up for them.

Nobody is signed in to Amit on this computer. Sign in to the Hub here first: a phone belongs to the person who pairs it.

What your phone gets

MouseMove the pointer, click, scroll and zoom with your finger. Touchpad or Direct.
NavigationYour own Helper buttons, the Helper bar, the Amit menu and the Aurora, one tap away.
PhotoTake a picture; it opens on the computer in Show Amit.
Ask AmitType or speak a question; the answer shows on the phone and can be spoken.
More will be added here as they are built.

Put it on your phone's home screen

  1. Pair the phone (right), then scan its code with the phone's camera.
  2. Open the link it gives you, then tap Share and Add to Home Screen.
  3. Next time, tap the Amit Pad icon. It opens full screen, with no browser bars, as long as this computer is on, the Helper is running and you are on the same Wi-Fi.
Away from home it will not connect yet; that needs the secure web address that is still to come.
The phone pad is ... for this computer.
Switching it off stops every paired phone at once. It does not unpair them.

Connect a phone

One press makes a private key for that one phone and shows its QR code below. After that the code stays here for that phone; you do not have to press anything again. Pair only phones that are yours.
