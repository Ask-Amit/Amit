Re: Character Reference for Adam Ackerman

To the Membership Committee:

I am writing at Adam Ackerman's request to recommend him for membership in the Eagles. I have known Adam for about two years, from the time he lived here in our community. I was glad to be part of his marriage to his wife, Brandy, and I have seen him as a friend, a neighbor, and a volunteer. I can speak to his character without hesitation.

When I owned a coffee shop, Adam volunteered his time with us. Nobody asked him to. If we were short-handed or a few of us were not feeling well, Adam filled in. He often showed up when he was not even on the schedule, and he served our customers in whatever way he was able, simply so each person who walked in felt welcomed and valued. He never asked for anything in return.

Adam faces some challenges of his own and he does not move as quickly as some. None of that has ever stopped him from giving of himself. He will do anything for anyone who needs help, and he does it with a good and open heart. In my experience that kind of generosity is rare, and he gives it without being asked and without keeping score.

I understand that the Eagles stand for people helping people, and for fellowship, service to the community, and good character. Adam already lives that way. He is honest, kind, dependable in his willingness, and he treats everyone with respect. The Eagles would be getting a member who cares about the people around him and shows up for them.

I recommend Adam Ackerman to you without reservation. If you would like to speak with me about him, I am happy to answer any questions.

Sincerely,
