Scope of Work
BOSS Inc. will perform a targeted repair and reinforcement of the existing metal roofing at 706 Main St. All existing nail fasteners across the roof structure will be removed and replaced with screws for a more secure, long-term hold. Three panels of metal roofing on the rear of the structure will be replaced with panels reclaimed from a separate BOSS Inc. project currently underway.
  • Remove all existing nail fasteners on the roof and replace with screws
  • Seal remaining loose fastening points with caulk
  • Replace three (3) metal roofing panels on the rear of the structure with reclaimed panels

03
Exclusions & Disclosures
Because this project reuses existing roofing material that has shown prior signs of water leakage, BOSS Inc. cannot guarantee a fully watertight result. Every reasonable effort will be made to achieve a watertight seal, but this outcome cannot be warrantied given the condition of the reused materials.
Per the owner's request, this scope does not include recoating the roof with Ameri-Coat or any similar elastomeric coating. A full roof recoat is excluded from this proposal and its pricing.
The three reclaimed replacement panels are green. The new screws used throughout will not match the color of the existing roof, as the remainder of the roof has faded over time. This is a cosmetic difference the owner should expect and has been made aware of in advance.
